How it scores

Points

Every flight earns 1 point per metre plus 3 points per second in the air. A personal best in distance or hang time adds 20. Setting a field record for farthest, longest aloft, highest or most loops adds 100 (50 for loops). Beating a real Guinness record (88.3 m, 29.2 s) adds 500.

Physics

Your creases are applied to the paper exactly: every layer on the folding side is mirrored across the crease. The finished stack is measured at 2 mm resolution to find the wing area, span, layer thickness, balance point and inertia. A 2D flight model then handles lift, drag, stall, pitching and tumbling every 3 ms. Roll and yaw aren't modelled, which is why folds are always mirrored.

Fairness

Everyone gets the same thrower and the same size of random wobble, seeded from the plane, so everyone watching sees the same throws. On a quiet field you can throw a plane a minute; when lots of pilots are throwing, the wait stretches towards an hour.
